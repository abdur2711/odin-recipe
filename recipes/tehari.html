Beef Tehari

Beef tehari is a fragrant Bangladeshi rice and beef dish cooked with mustard oil, whole and ground spices, yogurt, and green chilies. Unlike layered biryani, the rice is cooked together with the spiced beef and gravy.

Ingredients

  • Beef & Marinade: 600 g beef (cut into small bite-sized pieces, with a bit of fat or bone), 100 g plain yogurt, 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tsp red chili powder, 1 tsp turmeric powder, salt to taste.
  • Whole & Ground Spices: 100 ml mustard oil, 2 sliced onions, 1 bay leaf, 1 cinnamon stick, 3 green cardamom pods, 3 cloves, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp garam masala (optional: pinch of grated nutmeg and mace).
  • Rice & Finish: 500 g basmati or kalijeera rice (washed and soaked for 30 minutes, then drained), 700 ml water (adjust slightly to rice), 6 whole green chilies, 1 tsp kewra water (optional), 2 small potatoes halved (optional).

Instructions

  1. Marinate: Mix the beef with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, turmeric, and salt. Let it rest for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Sauté Aromatics & Beef: Heat mustard oil in a heavy pot. Fry the bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves until fragrant, then add sliced onions and cook until golden. Add the marinated beef and cumin, coriander, and garam masala; cook on medium-high heat for about 10 minutes until browned.
  3. Simmer Beef: Add potatoes if using. Pour in enough water to just cover the meat, cover, and simmer on low heat for about 40 minutes until tender.
  4. Cook Rice: Stir in the drained rice, whole green chilies, remaining water, and extra salt if needed. Bring to a boil.
  5. 1. Steam (Dum): Cover tightly and cook on very low heat for 18–20 minutes until the rice is tender and liquid is absorbed. Drizzle kewra water if using, turn off the heat, and let it rest covered for 10 minutes before gently fluffing.

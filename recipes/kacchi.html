Mutton Kacchi recipe

To make Mutton Kacchi Biryani, marinate raw bone-in mutton and layer it with parboiled basmati rice, fried onions (birista), and aromatic spices, then seal the pot with dough and slow-cook (dum) over low heat until the meat is tender and the rice is fragrant.

Ingredients

  • Mutton & Marinade: 1.5 kg bone-in mutton (large pieces, shoulder or leg), 1 cup full-fat yogurt (whisked), 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 1 tbsp red chili powder, 1/2 tsp turmeric, 2 tsp garam masala, 1/2 tsp nutmeg & mace powder, 1/2 cup fried onions (birista), 100 ml mustard or neutral oil, and salt to taste.
  • Rice & Parboiling: 600g–1 kg aged basmati rice (soaked 30–45 minutes), whole spices (cinnamon, green cardamom, cloves, bay leaves, shahi jeera), and 2.5–3 tbsp salt for boiling water.
  • Layering & Dum: 4 large potatoes (halved, salted, and lightly fried—optional), remaining birista, 1/3–1/2 cup ghee, saffron steeped in warm milk, 2 tsp kewra water or rose water, and flour dough for sealing.

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. 1. Marinate the mutton: Mix the mutton with yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spice powders, half the birista, oil, and salt. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (or overnight; avoid marinating overnight if using raw papaya paste) and bring to room temperature 45–60 minutes before cooking.
  2. Parboil the rice: Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil with salt and whole spices. Add drained rice and cook for 5–6 minutes until about 70% done (grains still have a firm/white center). Drain immediately and spread out to stop cooking.
  3. Layer the pot: Spread the marinated mutton in a single layer at the bottom of a heavy-bottomed pot. Add fried potatoes if using, then layer the parboiled rice on top. Scatter remaining birista, drizzle saffron milk, kewra or rose water, and melted ghee.
  4. Seal and slow-cook (dum): Roll flour dough into a rope to seal the lid tightly so no steam escapes. Cook on high heat for 10 minutes, then place over a tawa or heat diffuser on very low heat for 50–70 minutes (or bake at 150°C–165°C for 60–75 minutes).
  5. Rest and serve: Turn off the heat and let the sealed pot rest for 10–15 minutes. Break the seal, gently scoop from the bottom edge to combine meat and rice without breaking grains, and serve hot.

Homemade pizza

A classic homemade pizza is easy to make at home using simple ingredients for a crispy crust, rich tomato sauce, and melted cheese.

ingredients

Pizza Dough

  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 teaspoon active dry yeast or instant yeast
  • 1 teaspoon granulated sugar
  • 2 to 2 and 1/2 cups all-purpose flour or bread flower
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1 to 2 tablespoon olive oil

Sauce and toppings

  • ½ cup pizza sauce or crushed tomatoes (seasoned with garlic, salt, and oregano)
  • 1½ cups shredded or sliced mozzarella cheese
  • Your favorite toppings (pepperoni slices, mushrooms, bell peppers, or fresh basil)

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Activate the Yeast: In a large bowl, combine the warm water, sugar, and yeast. Let it sit for 5 to 7 minutes until it becomes foamy on top.
  2. Mix the Dough: Add the flour, salt, and olive oil to the yeast mixture. Stir until a shaggy dough ball forms, then knead on a lightly floured surface for 5 minutes until smooth and elastic.
  3. First Rise: Place the dough in an oiled bowl, cover it with a damp cloth or plastic wrap, and let it rise in a warm spot for about 1 hour until doubled in size.
  4. Shape the Crust: Preheat your oven to 450°F–500°F (230°C–260°C). Punch down the risen dough and stretch or roll it out into a 12- to 14-inch circle on a baking sheet, pizza stone, or cast-iron skillet.
  5. Add Toppings: Spread a thin layer of pizza sauce over the base, leaving a small border for the edge. Layer on the mozzarella cheese and your choice of toppings.
  6. Bake: Bake for 12 to 15 minutes (or 8 to 12 minutes on a hot stone) until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is hot and bubbly. Cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
